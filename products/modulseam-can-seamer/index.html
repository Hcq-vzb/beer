Two-roll seaming explained

The can is lifted into the chuck, the lid is pressed on, then a first-operation roll forms the hook and a second-operation roll tightens the seam to the target thickness. Roll pressure and chuck height are the two settings that decide whether the seam holds pressure, and both are locked and recorded per format on this machine.

Inline seam vision

A camera measures seam thickness, width and cover hook on every can. Out-of-tolerance cans are rejected by an air blast before they reach the packer, so a drifting roll does not turn into a pallet of leakers.

Pre-purge for oxygen-sensitive beer

An optional CO2 pre-purge station displaces air from the headspace before seaming. Combined with a short transfer from the filler, total package oxygen stays under 30 ppb, which is the practical threshold for hoppy beers to hold aroma for three months.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a separate depalletiser?

For under 6,000 cans per hour, cans can be loaded from a magazine by hand. Above that, an automatic depalletiser and twist rinser are recommended.

How often must seams be checked?

The inline vision covers every can. A manual tear-down check is still recommended at the start of each shift and after any roll adjustment.

Can it handle 202 CDL ends?

Yes, with the appropriate chuck and roll set. Specify the end type when ordering so the correct tooling ships with the machine.
