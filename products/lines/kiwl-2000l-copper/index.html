KIWL 2000 L Copper Brewhouse

A 2,000 L brewhouse finished in copper for visitor-facing sites, keeping the stainless interior that makes cleaning straightforward.

Key Specifications

Batch size2,000 L per batch
Vessel configuration3 or 4, copper clad
HeatingSteam 0.7 t/h
Material304 interior, 2 mm copper cladding
Brews per day4-5 per day
Footprint40 m2

Where the copper budget is best spent

Cladding the kettle and lauter tun, which guests actually see, costs far less than cladding the whole set including the whirlpool and HLT. On a 2,000 L system that choice alone saves a meaningful share of the premium.

Visitor-facing design constraints

A copper brewhouse in a taproom needs the chimney and condensate handling designed for the space, not just the vessel. Plan the flue route before the vessels are ordered.

Typical Applications

  • Breweries planning around a 2,000 batch — At 4-5 per day the brewhouse delivers roughly 20,000 hl of finished beer per year on a five-day week, which sets the cellar and packaging requirement downstream.
  • Sites with 40 of available floor space — The 3 or 4, copper clad layout fits within 40 m2, and the vessel arrangement determines how much of that space must stay clear for cleaning access.
  • Plants committed to steam heating — Steam 0.7 t/h is the practical choice here: it removes the boiler room below 2,000 L per batch, while larger sizes recover the cost of steam through faster ramp rates and tighter control.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can only part of the set be copper?

Yes, and it is common. Cladding the kettle and lauter tun while leaving the whirlpool and HLT stainless is the usual cost-balanced choice.

Does the copper change the beer?

No. All product contact is stainless; the copper is purely a shell finish.

What is included with the 2,000 configuration?

The supply covers the 3 or 4, copper clad set, interconnecting sanitary piping, the control panel and CIP routing, with Steam 0.7 t/h as specified. Cellar tanks, packaging and building works are separate scope, so the plant can be sized to your own distribution plan rather than a fixed package.
