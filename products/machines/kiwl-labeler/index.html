Label position is a tolerance, not an art

Retail audits measure label position against the container seam. A machine that holds ±1 mm passes consistently; one that drifts fails intermittently, which is worse because it is unpredictable. Servo-driven label transfer is what holds the tolerance across a run.

Wrap-around versus cold glue versus self-adhesive

Wrap-around gives the best coverage and cost per label at high volume. Cold glue suits paper labels on returnable bottles where the label must wash off. Self-adhesive needs no glue but costs more per label and is harder to remove for returnables.

Changeover time decides mixed-SKU economics

Storing a recipe per SKU and using quick-release plate holders brings changeover under 30 minutes. On a line running five SKUs a week that is several hours of capacity recovered.

Typical Applications

  • Breweries with retail label requirements — Retailers specify label position and content; ±1 mm accuracy keeps pallets from being rejected.
  • Breweries on returnable bottles — Cold glue labels wash off cleanly in the bottle washer, which is essential for a returnable loop.
  • Breweries running seasonal SKUs — Stored recipes and fast changeover make short seasonal runs economic.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can it apply neck and back labels?

Yes, with additional stations. A three-station configuration handles body, neck and back.

Does it work with cans?

Yes with can tooling. Sleeve application is a separate module.

How is label position verified?

An optional camera checks position and presence on every container and rejects out-of-tolerance units.
