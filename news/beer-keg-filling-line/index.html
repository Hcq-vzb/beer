Beer Keg Filling Line

Published 2026-11-27 · KIWL Machine

Three questions decide most beer keg filling line purchases, and they are the same for every craft and regional breweries operation: what rate, what accuracy, and what happens when it stops. This guide takes them in that order.

What This Equipment Does

A beer keg filling line occupies one defined station in the production line for craft and regional breweries. This build runs at 4464–5964 units per hour for 500 ml can product, and the configuration at that rate determines output consistency, labour and finished quality.

Key Technical Specifications

For a beer keg filling line aimed at craft and regional breweries operations, the practical reference build is 304/316L stainless steel contact parts, a 7-inch colour touch and a 3–4 kW connected load inside 1200 × 3600 × 2800 mm.

Integration With Your Line

Buffer sizing upstream of the beer keg filling line determines whether the line absorbs a changeover. With 4464–5964 bottles per hour and a 8-minute format change, the buffer has to cover the stop, not the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity is only worth what you can sell. A beer keg filling line at 4464–5964 bottles per hour should be sized against contracted demand for 500 ml can containers, otherwise the ROI depends on a market the craft and regional breweries operation does not control.

Maintenance and Service

Record every intervention against the beer keg filling line. After a few months the pattern of the seals and the drive tells you the real service interval, which is usually shorter than the manual claims.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the beer keg filling line need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1200 × 3600 × 2800 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

How long does a format changeover take on this beer keg filling line?

With a standardised guide set the changeover is around 8 minutes. The first change on a new 500 ml can format takes longer because the guide set has to be set and taught.

Can the beer keg filling line be upgraded to higher speed later?

Within the 4464–5964 band the drive and control have headroom. Moving beyond it means a different metering head, so plan the 500 ml can range before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
