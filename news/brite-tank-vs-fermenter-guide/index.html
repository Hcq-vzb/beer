Brite Tank vs Fermenter: Brewery Tank Guide

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Machine

Brite tanks and fermenters look similar but do different jobs. Understanding the brite tank vs fermenter distinction helps breweries size cellar capacity correctly and avoid the most common packaging bottleneck.

Different Jobs, Different Specs

Fermenters convert wort to beer with temperature control and blow-off; brite tanks condition, carbonate and hold finished beer under pressure ready for packaging.

Pressure and Cooling

Brite tanks are pressure-rated for carbonation with jacket or external cooling; fermenters prioritize cooling rate during active fermentation.

Sizing the Cellar

Plan brite volume at 1.5–2x weekly packaged output so beer has time to clear and carbonate before packaging.

Cleaning and Ports

Both need CIP spray devices; brite tanks add sample valves, carbonation stones and pressure relief for packaging operations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a unitank serve as both?

Unitanks combine both roles and suit breweries prioritizing footprint over flexibility.

How many brite tanks do I need?

Size for packaged volume plus one tank of buffer for seasonal and contract releases.

Do brite tanks need insulation?

Yes — jacketed and insulated tanks hold carbonation temperature and protect clarity.
