Brewery Whirlpool Tank

Published 2026-11-02 · KIWL Machine

Everything that follows concerns a single machine: a brewery whirlpool tank for craft and regional breweries. The rate, the materials, the control system, the changeover and the acceptance checks — presented so that a technical buyer and an operations manager can use the same document.

What This Equipment Does

For a craft and regional breweries operation, the brewery whirlpool tank is where a production target either becomes repeatable or stays theoretical. This unit runs at 560–810 units per hour on 8 oz product with a measured deviation rather than a quoted one.

Key Technical Specifications

Capacity without conditions is not a specification. 560–810 units per hour means little without 8 oz product, a 0.3 ml tolerance and a 10-minute changeover — all three stated for this brewery whirlpool tank.

Integration With Your Line

Buffer sizing upstream of the brewery whirlpool tank determines whether the line absorbs a changeover. With 560–810 units per hour and a 10-minute format change, the buffer has to cover the stop, not the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service arrangement in the numbers. A brewery whirlpool tank with a 4-inch HMI and a 10-minute changeover has a running cost that can be predicted, and predictability is worth more to a craft and regional breweries buyer than a lower price.

Maintenance and Service

Documentation is part of maintenance. Ask for the exploded view, the seal change procedure and the control parameter backup, because a brewery whirlpool tank with food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts and 560–810 units per hour is not a machine your team can improvise on.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 4-inch HMI stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for craft and regional breweries operations running audits.

What accuracy can this brewery whirlpool tank hold in real production?

The reference build holds 0.3 ml on 8 oz product at 560–810 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

Is a food-grade 304 stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 8 oz product the standard grade is not appropriate.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
