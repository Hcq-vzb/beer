Beer Bright Tank

Published 2026-10-25 · KIWL Machine

A beer bright tank sits on the critical path of the craft and regional breweries production line, which makes the selection decision more consequential than the specification sheet suggests. What follows is the working detail — capacity, materials, control architecture and the acceptance checks — for teams evaluating a beer bright tank.

What This Equipment Does

The test of a beer bright tank is not the demonstration but the third month. At 2436–3186 units per hour for 2 L product, this configuration is designed to hold its rate once the novelty of commissioning has worn off.

Key Technical Specifications

Rated capacity of 2436–3186 units per hour is only meaningful alongside the food-grade 304 stainless steel contact-part grade and the control system, because the cheaper version of each trades directly against accuracy.

Integration With Your Line

Physical integration is the easy half. The harder half is whether the craft and regional breweries team will adopt the routine — and a beer bright tank with a 10-minute changeover is far easier to adopt than one that needs a technician.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity above contracted demand is exposure, not return. Size the beer bright tank to real 2 L volume, then let the 2.0 ml tolerance and the 2436–3186 units per hour figure do the work on yield.

Maintenance and Service

The wear items on this beer bright tank are the seals, the drive and the guide rails. None of them should fail without warning, and none of them should require a week of lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 7-inch colour touch stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for craft and regional breweries operations running audits.

How long does a format changeover take on this beer bright tank?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 10 minutes. The first change to a new 2 L format takes longer.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?
