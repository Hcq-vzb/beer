Brewery Cleaning System

Published 2026-11-14 · KIWL Machine

Three questions decide most brewery cleaning system purchases, and they are the same for every craft and regional breweries operation: what rate, what accuracy, and what happens when it stops. This guide takes them in that order.

What This Equipment Does

A brewery cleaning system earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 616–866 units per hour for 500 ml can product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

Every quotation for a brewery cleaning system contains a headline rate and a long list of options. The four entries that actually determine the outcome are the 0.5 ml tolerance, the food-grade 304 stainless steel contact-part grade, the 15–22 kW load and the 1600 × 1900 × 2100 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

A brewery cleaning system fits an existing craft and regional breweries production line when three things align: the transfer pitch, the control protocol and the cleaning route. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and works across a 1600 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Look at the brewery cleaning system over five years rather than one budget cycle. food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts, a 0.5 ml tolerance and a 20-minute changeover have different cost curves, and the purchase price tells you very little about any of them.

Maintenance and Service

A brewery cleaning system that is easy to maintain is one your team will actually maintain. On this build the wear items are visible, the intervals are published and the reset procedure fits on one page.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

How is the brewery cleaning system documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
