Beer Packaging Line

Published 2026-11-08 · KIWL Machine

This guide is written for the person who signs the order and the person who will run the machine. Both need the same craft and regional breweries figures, presented so that neither has to translate them first.

What This Equipment Does

The unit a beer packaging line performs is easy to describe and hard to do well: it takes 1 L product and delivers 1572–2222 units per hour, and everything downstream is calibrated against that figure.

Key Technical Specifications

A beer packaging line is easy to compare when four numbers are isolated from the option list. Here they are: ±1.5 ml, 1572–2222 units per hour, 20–27 kW, and 1800 × 1900 × 2800 mm, on 1 L product.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The beer packaging line is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the craft and regional breweries workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity above contracted demand is exposure, not return. Size the beer packaging line to real 1 L volume, then let the 1.5 ml tolerance and the 1572–2222 units per hour figure do the work on yield.

Maintenance and Service

Ask the supplier to run a maintenance walkthrough with the shift team before the beer packaging line leaves the factory. On this build the routine points — seals, drive, guide rails — are reachable with ordinary tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this beer packaging line hold in real production?

The reference build holds 1.5 ml on 1 L product at 1572–2222 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

Can the beer packaging line handle a 1 L product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 20-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 4-inch HMI.

What is the actual connected load of the beer packaging line?

The reference build draws 20–27 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
