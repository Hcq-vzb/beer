Yeast Brink Tank

Published 2026-11-18 · KIWL Machine

Two yeast brink tank quotations can quote the same rate and still describe different machines. This guide covers the craft and regional breweries details that tell them apart — accuracy, materials, control and changeover — and what to write into the order.

What This Equipment Does

The value of a yeast brink tank is consistency across shifts. This configuration holds 2405–3155 units per hour on 60–120 mm diameter product, so planning does not have to assume the worst week of the year.

Key Technical Specifications

Do not compare a yeast brink tank on rated capacity alone. A 2405–3155 units per hour machine with a 2.0 ml tolerance and 304/316L stainless steel contact parts will out-produce a faster machine with looser accuracy over a full shift.

Integration With Your Line

A yeast brink tank fits an existing craft and regional breweries production line when three things align: the transfer pitch, the control protocol and the cleaning route. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and works across a 1600 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest ROI argument for a yeast brink tank is downtime avoided during a 12-minute format change on a 60–120 mm diameter format, because the craft and regional breweries production line loses output that cannot be recovered later in the day.

Maintenance and Service

Serviceability is a specification, not an afterthought. On this yeast brink tank the seals, sensors and drives are accessible without dismantling the frame, which is what makes a 12-minute routine service realistic on a 2405 unit per hour line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the yeast brink tank need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1600 × 1400 × 2100 mm GA drawing.

Is a 304/316L stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 60–120 mm diameter product the standard grade is not appropriate.

Can the yeast brink tank be upgraded later?

Within the 2405–3155 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 60–120 mm diameter range before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
