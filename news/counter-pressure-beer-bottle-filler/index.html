Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the counter pressure beer bottle filler?

Seals, drive belt and guide rails are the wear items. For a line running 3089 bottles per hour, holding these on site is what protects availability.

Does the counter pressure beer bottle filler need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 2400 × 1400 × 2400 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

How long does a format changeover take on this counter pressure beer bottle filler?

With a standardised guide set the changeover is around 8 minutes. The first change on a new 500 ml can format takes longer because the guide set has to be set and taught.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
