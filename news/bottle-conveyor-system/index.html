Bottle Conveyor System

Published 2026-11-15 · KIWL Machine

Two bottle conveyor system quotations can quote the same rate and still describe different machines. This guide covers the craft and regional breweries details that tell them apart — accuracy, materials, control and changeover — and what to write into the order.

What This Equipment Does

The value of a bottle conveyor system is consistency across shifts. This configuration holds 16447–20947 units per hour on 20 L product, so planning does not have to assume the worst week of the year.

Key Technical Specifications

For a bottle conveyor system aimed at craft and regional breweries operations, the practical reference build is SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts, a 4-inch HMI and a 18–25 kW connected load inside 2400 × 1900 × 2400 mm.

Integration With Your Line

The upstream buffer decides how much downtime a bottle conveyor system can absorb. With 16447–20947 units per hour and a 10-minute changeover, the buffer has to cover the change rather than the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Payback comes from three places: fewer manual steps, less give-away, less downtime. A bottle conveyor system handling 16447–20947 units per hour with a 1.0 ml tolerance attacks all three for a craft and regional breweries operation.

Maintenance and Service

A bottle conveyor system that is easy to maintain is one your team will actually maintain. On this build the wear items are visible, the intervals are published and the reset procedure fits on one page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the bottle conveyor system handle a 20 L product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 10-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 4-inch HMI.

How long does a format changeover take on this bottle conveyor system?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 10 minutes. The first change to a new 20 L format takes longer.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
