Brewery Fermentation Controller

Published 2026-11-17 · KIWL Machine

The figures in this guide are the ones a supplier should be held to in writing. For a brewery fermentation controller in craft and regional breweries production, they cover rate, tolerance, materials, control, changeover and acceptance.

What This Equipment Does

A brewery fermentation controller occupies one defined station in the production line for craft and regional breweries. This build runs at 629–879 units per hour for 8 oz product, and the configuration at that rate determines output consistency, labour and finished quality.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask for the electrical single-line diagram and the duty cycle, not only the power total. This brewery fermentation controller draws 16–23 kW across a 2400 × 1900 × 2800 mm footprint with 304 stainless steel contact parts.

Integration With Your Line

The best commissioning plan names three people: one for the 1.5 ml verification, one for the encoder handshake, one for the cleaning route. With a 629–879 units per hour machine, that is what a week-long delay costs.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest ROI argument for a brewery fermentation controller is downtime avoided during a 20-minute format change on a 8 oz format, because the craft and regional breweries production line loses output that cannot be recovered later in the day.

Maintenance and Service

Documentation is part of maintenance. Ask for the exploded view, the seal change procedure and the control parameter backup, because a brewery fermentation controller with 304 stainless steel contact parts and 629–879 units per hour is not a machine your team can improvise on.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the brewery fermentation controller do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 7-inch colour touch stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for craft and regional breweries operations running audits.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
