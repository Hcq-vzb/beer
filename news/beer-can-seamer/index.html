Beer Can Seamer

Published 2026-10-29 · KIWL Machine

There is no universal beer can seamer for craft and regional breweries. The correct configuration depends on the can format, the lid type and the campaign pattern. This guide walks through the decision in the order a buyer makes it: what the machine must do, what it must be made of, what it costs to run.

What This Equipment Does

Repeatability is the beer can seamer's job. This configuration seams 330 ml can format at 5529–7029 cans per minute with a 3.0–4.5 N·m window, which means the result does not depend on which operator is on shift.

Key Technical Specifications

Capacity without conditions is not a specification. 5529–7029 units per hour means little without 330 ml can product, a 3.0 ml tolerance and a 8-minute changeover — all three stated for this beer can seamer.

Integration With Your Line

The beer can seamer has to be locked to the line, not placed near it. This build accepts a line encoder signal, so 5529–7029 cans per minute arrives in phase with the upstream craft and regional breweries conveyor.

Capacity Planning and ROI

A beer can seamer returns value in two places: rejects that stop and changeovers that shorten. Holding 3.0–4.5 N·m at 5529–7029 cans per minute removes the first, and a 8-minute format change attacks the second.

Maintenance and Service

A beer can seamer that is easy to maintain is one your team will actually maintain. On this build the wear items are visible, the intervals are published and the reset procedure fits on one page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the servo settings be backed up?

Yes. The 10-inch colour touch stores recipes per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for craft and regional breweries operationss running several 330 ml can format.

How does the beer can seamer integrate with an existing line?

It accepts a line encoder signal and can be indexed directly against the upstream conveyor, so it runs under the line PLC rather than as an isolated unit.

What is the connected load of the beer can seamer?

The reference build draws 6.5–8.5 kW with a servo rotary head. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
