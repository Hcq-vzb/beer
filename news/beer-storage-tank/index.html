Beer Storage Tank

Published 2026-11-13 · KIWL Machine

Before any beer storage tank quotation is compared, the rate has to be tied to a product and a format. This guide does that for craft and regional breweries operations and then works through the rest of the decision.

What This Equipment Does

A beer storage tank earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 502–752 units per hour for 16 oz product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare rated capacity, contact-part material, control architecture and changeover time rather than headline speed. This reference beer storage tank uses 316L stainless steel for product-contact surfaces, a 4-inch HMI, a 21–28 kW connected load and a 1200 × 2200 × 2100 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The beer storage tank is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the craft and regional breweries workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the beer storage tank on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 16 oz product with a 15-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

The beer storage tank was specified with wear part lead times in mind, so a craft and regional breweries operation is not left waiting a week for a seal while the line stands at 502 units per hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

What accuracy can this beer storage tank hold in real production?

The reference build holds 1.0 ml on 16 oz product at 502–752 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

Does the beer storage tank need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1200 × 2200 × 2100 mm GA drawing.

What spare parts should be held for the beer storage tank?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 502 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
