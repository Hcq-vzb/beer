Mash Filter vs Lauter Tun

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machine

Choosing between a mash filter and a lauter tun defines your brewhouse footprint and rhythm. This comparison breaks down the trade-offs for new and expanding breweries.

How Each Separates

Lauter tuns use the grain bed as the filter medium over a false bottom; mash filters press mash between cloth and plates for fast, bed-free runoff.

Cycle Time and Footprint

Mash filters turn faster and occupy less floor space per brew; lauter tuns need larger diameter but tolerate rushed or imperfect mashes.

Extract and Water Use

Mash filters typically deliver higher extract and lower water per brew; lauter tuns offer more forgiving sparging for varied grists.

Best Fit by Scale

Lauter tuns dominate craft and mid-scale breweries; mash filters win in high-frequency, space-constrained industrial houses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is easier for a first brewery?

Lauter tuns are more forgiving of milling variation and operator learning curves.

Do mash filters handle raw grain?

They tolerate adjuncts and high-adjunct bills better than most lauter tun setups.

Can I switch later?

Separation vessels are a core brewhouse decision — plan for the next five years of volume before choosing.
