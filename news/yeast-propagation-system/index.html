Yeast Propagation System

Published 2026-11-01 · KIWL Machine

There is a short answer and a long answer to specifying a yeast propagation system. The short one is the rate; the long one — and the one that determines whether the craft and regional breweries production line runs — is everything the rate depends on.

What This Equipment Does

The test of a yeast propagation system is not the demonstration but the third month. At 13612–17612 units per hour for 500 ml product, this configuration is designed to hold its rate once the novelty of commissioning has worn off.

Key Technical Specifications

This yeast propagation system draws 14–19 kW and occupies 1600 × 3600 × 3200 mm, uses SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts and holds a 0.5 ml tolerance on 500 ml product.

Integration With Your Line

A yeast propagation system that runs beside the line rather than inside it will always be the bottleneck. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and a 1600 mm working width, which is what lets the craft and regional breweries production line treat it as one controlled step.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare a yeast propagation system against the cost of the alternative rather than against zero. The manual step it removes at 13612 units per hour usually outweighs the purchase price inside the first operating year for a craft and regional breweries operation.

Maintenance and Service

The yeast propagation system keeps its wear items accessible and its parameters exportable, so a shift leader can restore a setting without calling a technician and without losing 13612 units per hour of output.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the yeast propagation system run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 10-inch colour touch, yes. The craft and regional breweries team still owns the routine checks, but the 15-minute changeover is guided.

What does the yeast propagation system do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
