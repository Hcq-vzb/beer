Craft Beer Canning Line Selection Guide

Published 2026-06-14 · KIWL Machine

Understanding craft beer canning line requirements helps plant managers and buyers make confident equipment decisions. This guide summarizes practical criteria aligned with current industry practice.

Why Microbreweries Invest in Canning Lines

Craft breweries moving beyond taproom sales need reliable canning to reach retail and e-commerce channels. A well-matched craft beer canning line reduces oxygen pickup, stabilizes flavor, and supports consistent seam quality for aluminum cans.

Key Specifications to Compare

Evaluate rated output (CPM), can height range, rinser type, counter-pressure filler technology, and seamer integration. Monoblock designs save floor space; rotary fillers suit higher volumes above 60 CPM.

CO2 Recovery and Product Loss

Counter-pressure filling minimizes foam and oxygen ingress. Ask suppliers about pre-purge cycles, bowl pressure control, and typical product loss per shift — critical for margin on small-batch SKUs.

Integration with Brewing Equipment

Plan buffer tanks, bright beer lines, and CIP compatibility. Canning lines should match your cellar workflow so packaging does not become the bottleneck on brew days.

Frequently Asked Questions

What capacity should a startup brewery target?

Many microbreweries begin at 30–40 CPM with room to upgrade seamer or filler modules as demand grows.

Are canning lines suitable for nitro beers?

Yes, with specialized gas dosing and modified filling parameters — confirm with your equipment supplier.

How long is typical installation?

Compact lines often commission in 2–4 weeks including operator training.
