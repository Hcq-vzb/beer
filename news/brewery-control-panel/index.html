Brewery Control Panel

Published 2026-11-07 · KIWL Machine

The brewery control panel is easier to specify than most craft and regional breweries buyers expect, because the hard questions have already been asked by other plants. This guide sets out those questions and the answers, in the form of figures you can hold a supplier to.

What This Equipment Does

A brewery control panel earns a place on the craft and regional breweries production line by removing variation rather than by adding capacity. This build holds 1831–2481 units per hour on 8 oz product, which is what makes downstream planning possible.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that gets missed is the changeover. A brewery control panel with a 10-minute changeover across 8 oz format and SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts is a different proposition from one without, even at the same 1831–2481 units per hour.

Integration With Your Line

The upstream buffer decides how much downtime a brewery control panel can absorb. With 1831–2481 units per hour and a 10-minute changeover, the buffer has to cover the change rather than the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service contract in the ROI. A brewery control panel with a 7-inch colour touch and a 10-minute changeover loses far less output when a fault occurs, because the diagnosis is quicker and the reset is shorter.

Maintenance and Service

Ask the supplier to run a maintenance walkthrough with the shift team before the brewery control panel leaves the factory. On this build the routine points — seals, drive, guide rails — are reachable with ordinary tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the brewery control panel suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 1831–2481 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

Can the brewery control panel run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 7-inch colour touch, yes. The craft and regional breweries team still owns the routine checks, but the 10-minute changeover is guided.

What is the actual connected load of the brewery control panel?

The reference build draws 43–58 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
