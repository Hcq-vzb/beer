Small Brewery Packaging Line Layout

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Machine

A smart packaging line layout keeps a small brewery efficient as volume grows. This article covers flow, ergonomics and expansion planning.

One-Directional Flow

Arrange depalletizer, filler, seamer, tunnel pasteurizer, labeler and packer in a straight, logical path to cut forklift travel.

Ergonomics and Access

Keep change parts, CIP hooks and control panels at operator height with clear sightlines to every station.

Future Expansion Slots

Reserve floor space and utility drops for a second filler or case packer before you need them.

Common Mistakes

Under-sizing CIP and cellar upstream, and skipping pack-off buffer, are the two issues that stall lines most often.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much space do I need?

A compact canning cell can fit in under 60 m2 with straight flow.

Should pasteurizer come before labeler?

Yes — label after thermal treatment to avoid adhesive issues.

Can layout phase with budget?

Start with filler plus seamer, add pasteurizer and packer as volume justifies.
