Can Depalletizer

Published 2026-11-09 · KIWL Machine

Three questions decide most can depalletizer purchases, and they are the same for every craft and regional breweries operation: what rate, what accuracy, and what happens when it stops. This guide takes them in that order.

What This Equipment Does

Position a can depalletizer by what it removes from the craft and regional breweries process. This configuration handles 330 ml can product at 5116–6616 units per hour, which is the number the rest of the line is designed around.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that gets missed is the changeover. A can depalletizer with a 20-minute changeover across 330 ml can format and 304 stainless steel contact parts is a different proposition from one without, even at the same 5116–6616 units per hour.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The can depalletizer is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the craft and regional breweries workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service contract in the ROI. A can depalletizer with a 10-inch colour touch and a 20-minute changeover loses far less output when a fault occurs, because the diagnosis is quicker and the reset is shorter.

Maintenance and Service

A can depalletizer that is easy to maintain is one your team will actually maintain. On this build the wear items are visible, the intervals are published and the reset procedure fits on one page.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the can depalletizer documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

What spare parts should be held for the can depalletizer?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 5116 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

Can the can depalletizer run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 10-inch colour touch, yes. The craft and regional breweries team still owns the routine checks, but the 20-minute changeover is guided.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
