Craft Brewery Startup Equipment Checklist

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Machine

Launching a brewery means sequencing purchases so every dollar works before the next system arrives. This craft brewery startup equipment checklist organizes the must-haves by phase.

Phase 1: Brewhouse and Cellar

Mash-lauter-tun or two-vessel brewhouse, fermenters, brite tank, glycol chiller and CIP skid form the minimum viable production core.

Phase 2: Packaging

Add keg filler or bottling line once beer flows; keep bright beer lines CIP-compatible so packaging retrofits cleanly.

Phase 3: Utilities and Lab

Water treatment, boiler or electric heating, compressed air, plus a basic lab kit (DO meter, refractometer, pH meter) for quality control.

Budget Traps to Avoid

Under-sized chillers, missing buffer tanks and deferred water treatment are the three purchases most often regretted and re-bought.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum viable brewery?

A brewhouse, two to three fermenters, one brite tank, chiller and CIP skid cover first production.

Can I start contract brewing?

Yes — contract brewing defers equipment capex but limits recipe control and margins.

How much space for a nano brewery?

A focused nano operation often fits in 150–300 m2 including cellar and packaging.
