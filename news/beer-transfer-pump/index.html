Beer Transfer Pump

Published 2026-11-03 · KIWL Machine

The purpose of this guide is to shorten the beer transfer pump decision for a craft and regional breweries buyer. Rate first, then the specification points, then the acceptance checks that turn a quotation into a working machine.

What This Equipment Does

A beer transfer pump occupies one defined station, and the value of that station is decided by how little attention it needs. This craft and regional breweries build runs at 19318–23818 units per hour on 250–500 ml product and is specified so a shift operator can run it without specialist knowledge.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that limits output on a beer transfer pump is usually changeover. At 15 minutes per 250–500 ml format and 19318–23818 units per hour, campaigns have to be planned around the stop.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The beer transfer pump is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the craft and regional breweries workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Payback comes from three places: fewer manual steps, less give-away, less downtime. A beer transfer pump handling 19318–23818 units per hour with a 0.3 ml tolerance attacks all three for a craft and regional breweries operation.

Maintenance and Service

Ask the supplier to run a maintenance walkthrough with the shift team before the beer transfer pump leaves the factory. On this build the routine points — seals, drive, guide rails — are reachable with ordinary tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the beer transfer pump run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 10-inch colour touch, yes. The craft and regional breweries team still owns the routine checks, but the 15-minute changeover is guided.

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 10-inch colour touch stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for craft and regional breweries operations running audits.

How does the beer transfer pump behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the beer transfer pump determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
