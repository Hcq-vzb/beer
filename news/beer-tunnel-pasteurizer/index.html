Beer Tunnel Pasteurizer

Published 2026-11-20 · KIWL Machine

The figures in this guide are the ones a supplier should be held to in writing. For a beer tunnel pasteurizer in craft and regional breweries production, they cover rate, tolerance, materials, control, changeover and acceptance.

What This Equipment Does

The test of a beer tunnel pasteurizer is not the demonstration but the third month. At 10078–13578 units per hour for 330 ml product, this configuration is designed to hold its rate once the novelty of commissioning has worn off.

Key Technical Specifications

A beer tunnel pasteurizer is properly specified when an auditor can check it. Rated capacity 10078–13578 units per hour, contact parts 316L stainless steel, control 10-inch colour touch, load 5.5–7.5 kW, footprint 1600 × 2800 × 2400 mm, changeover 20 minutes — this build carries all six.

Integration With Your Line

The costliest mistake is treating a beer tunnel pasteurizer as a standalone machine. Once it accepts a line encoder signal and works across a 1600 mm working width, it becomes part of the craft and regional breweries control loop and recipe changes stop being manual.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service contract in the ROI. A beer tunnel pasteurizer with a 10-inch colour touch and a 20-minute changeover loses far less output when a fault occurs, because the diagnosis is quicker and the reset is shorter.

Maintenance and Service

The parts worth stocking for a beer tunnel pasteurizer are small, common and cheap — seals, guide rails, sensor sets. Holding them turns a craft and regional breweries shutdown into a shift adjustment instead of a week of waiting.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the beer tunnel pasteurizer documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

Can the beer tunnel pasteurizer handle a 330 ml product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 20-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 10-inch colour touch.

Does the beer tunnel pasteurizer need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1600 × 2800 × 2400 mm GA drawing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
