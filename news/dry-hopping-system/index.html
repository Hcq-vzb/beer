Dry Hopping System

Published 2026-11-06 · KIWL Machine

The dry hopping system is easier to specify than most craft and regional breweries buyers expect, because the hard questions have already been asked by other plants. This guide sets out those questions and the answers, in the form of figures you can hold a supplier to.

What This Equipment Does

The test of a dry hopping system is not the demonstration but the third month. At 2368–3368 units per hour for 1.5 L product, this configuration is designed to hold its rate once the novelty of commissioning has worn off.

Key Technical Specifications

A dry hopping system is easy to compare when four numbers are isolated from the option list. Here they are: ±3.0 ml, 2368–3368 units per hour, 9.5–12.5 kW, and 1200 × 3600 × 3200 mm, on 1.5 L product.

Integration With Your Line

The best commissioning plan names three people: one for the 3.0 ml verification, one for the encoder handshake, one for the cleaning route. With a 2368–3368 units per hour machine, that is what a week-long delay costs.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service arrangement in the numbers. A dry hopping system with a 10-inch colour touch and a 15-minute changeover has a running cost that can be predicted, and predictability is worth more to a craft and regional breweries buyer than a lower price.

Maintenance and Service

Treat the dry hopping system service records as production data. Once a few months of interventions are logged, the failure pattern on the seals and the drive usually tells you where the real risk sits.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the delivery lead time for the dry hopping system?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 1.5 L set is included.

What spare parts should be held for the dry hopping system?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 2368 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
