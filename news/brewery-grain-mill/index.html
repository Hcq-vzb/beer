What This Equipment Does

A brewery grain mill earns a place on the craft and regional breweries production line by removing variation rather than by adding capacity. This build holds 8757–11757 units per hour on 20 L product, which is what makes downstream planning possible.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that limits output on a brewery grain mill is usually changeover. At 20 minutes per 20 L format and 8757–11757 units per hour, campaigns have to be planned around the stop.

Integration With Your Line

Capacity Planning and ROI

A 20-minute changeover should be priced into the ROI, because on the craft and regional breweries production line that switches formats often it represents output that is otherwise lost rather than merely inconvenient.

Maintenance and Service

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the brewery grain mill be upgraded later?

Within the 8757–11757 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 20 L range before ordering.

What does the brewery grain mill do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How is the brewery grain mill documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
