Brewery CIP Pump

Published 2026-11-19 · KIWL Machine

The brewery CIP pump is easier to specify than most craft and regional breweries buyers expect, because the hard questions have already been asked by other plants. This guide sets out those questions and the answers, in the form of figures you can hold a supplier to.

What This Equipment Does

Position a brewery CIP pump by what it removes from the craft and regional breweries process. This configuration handles 16 oz product at 413–563 units per hour, which is the number the rest of the line is designed around.

Key Technical Specifications

Rated capacity of 413–563 units per hour is only meaningful alongside the food-grade 304 stainless steel contact-part grade and the control system, because the cheaper version of each trades directly against accuracy.

Integration With Your Line

Downstream transfer matters as much as upstream buffer. If the brewery CIP pump can hand over at 413–563 units per hour without accumulation, the rest of the line keeps its own cadence.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Look at the brewery CIP pump over five years rather than one budget cycle. food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts, a 2.0 ml tolerance and a 8-minute changeover have different cost curves, and the purchase price tells you very little about any of them.

Maintenance and Service

Put the brewery CIP pump on a preventive schedule that counts cycles rather than calendar months. At 413–563 units per hour the seals reach end of life long before a quarterly inspection notices.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the brewery CIP pump do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How long does a format changeover take on this brewery CIP pump?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 8 minutes. The first change to a new 16 oz format takes longer.

Is the brewery CIP pump suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 413–563 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
