Kegging Line Automation for Microbreweries

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Machine

An automated kegging line replaces manual filling with controlled purge, fill and seal steps that protect beer from oxygen and cut labor. This guide covers what to specify.

Purge Before Fill

CO2 pre-purge of each keg displaces oxygen; automated lines hold purge time and pressure consistently batch to batch.

Filler Heads and Speed

Single-head semi-auto suits low volume; multi-head rotary lines serve regional distribution with fewer operators.

Integrated Cleaning

Built-in keg washer and CIP tie-in keep turnaround fast and sanitary between fills.

Labor and ROI

Automation typically pays back by removing 1–2 manual roles per shift while improving consistency.

Frequently Asked Questions

What volume justifies automation?

Beyond ~20 kegs per shift, semi-auto pays; regional scale warrants multi-head.

Does it handle different keg types?

Quick-change spears adapt to Euro, DIN and Cornelius kegs.

Is it linked to the brewhouse CIP?

It shares the same CIP supply as the rest of the line.
