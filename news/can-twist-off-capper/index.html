Can Twist Off Capper

Published 2026-11-23 · KIWL Machine

Specifying a can twist off capper for craft and regional breweries is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

What a craft and regional breweries buyer should verify on a can twist off capper is the torque window at the slowest speed as well as the fastest — the 3.0–4.5 N·m figure has to hold at 5537 cans per minute.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask what a supplier is not willing to write down. The answer, on a can twist off capper, is usually the tolerance at the low end of the range: this build holds 3.0 ml at 5537 units per hour, not only at 7037.

Integration With Your Line

The can twist off capper has to be indexed against the upstream line, not merely placed near it. This build accepts a line encoder signal, so it can run locked to the conveyor at 5537–7037 cans per minute.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model payback on the 12 oz format changeover frequency. A can twist off capper that changes format in 12 minutes turns a craft and regional breweries scheduling problem into a routine operation, and that is where the return shows up.

Maintenance and Service

A can twist off capper that is easy to maintain is one your team will actually maintain. On this build the wear items are visible, the intervals are published and the reset procedure fits on one page.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a format changeover take?

Around 12 minutes with standardised tooling. Budget more for the first change to a new 12 oz format until the tooling set is dialled in.

How does the can twist off capper integrate with an existing line?

It accepts a line encoder signal and can be indexed directly against the upstream conveyor, so it runs under the line PLC rather than as an isolated unit.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
