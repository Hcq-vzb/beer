Brewery Heat Exchanger

Published 2026-11-04 · KIWL Machine

A brewery heat exchanger for craft and regional breweries is judged on the specification and then lived with for years. This guide covers what to ask for, what to verify on the floor, and what to keep in stock afterwards.

What This Equipment Does

Because a brewery heat exchanger handles a defined step for craft and regional breweries, it can be isolated, measured and improved. This build is specified for 500 ml can product at 18526–23026 units per hour, with changeover handled by configuration rather than by hand.

Key Technical Specifications

Capacity without conditions is not a specification. 18526–23026 units per hour means little without 500 ml can product, a 1.0 ml tolerance and a 8-minute changeover — all three stated for this brewery heat exchanger.

Integration With Your Line

Integration work on a brewery heat exchanger is mostly deciding who owns what. Once the encoder, the cleaning route and the recipe store are agreed with the craft and regional breweries production line, commissioning stops being a negotiation.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the brewery heat exchanger on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 500 ml can product with a 8-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

The wear items on this brewery heat exchanger are the seals, the drive and the guide rails. None of them should fail without warning, and none of them should require a week of lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a format changeover take on this brewery heat exchanger?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 8 minutes. The first change to a new 500 ml can format takes longer.

What spare parts should be held for the brewery heat exchanger?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 18526 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How does the brewery heat exchanger behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the brewery heat exchanger determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
