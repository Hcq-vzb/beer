Brewery Kegging System

Published 2026-11-11 · KIWL Machine

Everything that follows concerns a single machine: a brewery kegging system for craft and regional breweries. The rate, the materials, the control system, the changeover and the acceptance checks — presented so that a technical buyer and an operations manager can use the same document.

What This Equipment Does

A brewery kegging system earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 1365–2015 units per hour for 500 ml product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare rated capacity, contact-part material, control architecture and changeover time rather than headline speed. This reference brewery kegging system uses food-grade 304 stainless steel for product-contact surfaces, a 7-inch colour touch, a 1.5–2.5 kW connected load and a 1600 × 1900 × 3200 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Physical integration is the easy half. The harder half is whether the craft and regional breweries team will adopt the routine — and a brewery kegging system with a 12-minute changeover is far easier to adopt than one that needs a technician.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Run the ROI on the worst month, not the average one. A brewery kegging system rated at 1365–2015 units per hour will look different in peak season than in a quiet month, and the investment case has to survive both.

Maintenance and Service

The brewery kegging system keeps its wear items accessible and its parameters exportable, so a shift leader can restore a setting without calling a technician and without losing 1365 units per hour of output.

Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the brewery kegging system?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 1365 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

Is the brewery kegging system suitable for continuous three-shift running?

The reference build is rated for continuous duty in the 1365–2015 band. For heavier duty ask for the upgraded drive package.

What does the brewery kegging system do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
