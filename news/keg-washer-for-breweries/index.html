Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a format changeover take on this keg washer for breweries?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 12 minutes. The first change to a new 330 ml can format takes longer.

Can the keg washer for breweries handle a 330 ml can product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 12-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 4-inch HMI.

How do I size this for my plant?

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.
