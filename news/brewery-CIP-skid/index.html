Brewery CIP Skid

Published 2026-10-28 · KIWL Machine

The difference between a workable brewery CIP skid and an expensive mistake usually shows up six months later, in downtime and in service calls. This guide covers the specification points that decide the outcome for craft and regional breweries, in the order they are best resolved.

What This Equipment Does

A brewery CIP skid earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 2512–3262 units per hour for 1.5 L product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

Do not compare a brewery CIP skid on rated capacity alone. A 2512–3262 units per hour machine with a 1.0 ml tolerance and SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts will out-produce a faster machine with looser accuracy over a full shift.

Integration With Your Line

The upstream buffer decides how much downtime a brewery CIP skid can absorb. With 2512–3262 units per hour and a 12-minute changeover, the buffer has to cover the change rather than the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service arrangement in the numbers. A brewery CIP skid with a 7-inch colour touch and a 12-minute changeover has a running cost that can be predicted, and predictability is worth more to a craft and regional breweries buyer than a lower price.

Maintenance and Service

Every intervention on the brewery CIP skid should be recorded against the unit serial and the hour meter. Six months of that data gives a craft and regional breweries operation its real service intervals for the SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 1.5 L product the standard grade is not appropriate.

How does the brewery CIP skid behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the brewery CIP skid determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

What does the brewery CIP skid do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
