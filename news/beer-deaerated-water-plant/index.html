Published 2026-11-25 · KIWL Machine

Three questions decide most beer deaerated water plant purchases, and they are the same for every craft and regional breweries operation: what rate, what accuracy, and what happens when it stops. This guide takes them in that order.

What This Equipment Does

A beer deaerated water plant should be judged on the range it handles, not the peak it advertises. This configuration covers 330 ml product at 16945–21445 units per hour, which is the span most craft and regional breweries production lines actually demand.

Key Technical Specifications

Read a beer deaerated water plant specification for what is measurable: rate 16945–21445 units per hour, deviation ±2.0 ml, container 330 ml, control 7-inch colour touch, load 3–4 kW, footprint 3200 × 1400 × 2400 mm, changeover 10 minutes. This build is quoted that way.

Integration With Your Line

Buffer sizing upstream of the beer deaerated water plant determines whether the line absorbs a changeover. With 16945–21445 units per hour and a 10-minute format change, the buffer has to cover the stop, not the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the beer deaerated water plant on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 330 ml product with a 10-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

The parts worth stocking for a beer deaerated water plant are small, common and cheap — seals, guide rails, sensor sets. Holding them turns a craft and regional breweries shutdown into a shift adjustment instead of a week of waiting.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a format changeover take on this beer deaerated water plant?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 10 minutes. The first change to a new 330 ml format takes longer.

How is the beer deaerated water plant documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

Can the beer deaerated water plant run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 7-inch colour touch, yes. The craft and regional breweries team still owns the routine checks, but the 10-minute changeover is guided.

How do I size this for my plant?

Does it integrate with existing lines?
